# Field Notes

Short, evidence-based write-ups from 14 years of enterprise pre-sales and customer success — mostly on what actually gets in the way of AI adoption at Korean enterprises, and what has worked.

Written by [Chanyeon Kim](https://github.com/chanyeonkim-lab). All notes are in English.

## Notes

- **[Hyundai Motor Application Global Assessment](./2026-09-crm-maturity-global-rollout.md)** — an executive-commissioned CRM maturity assessment across Hyundai Motor Group's country entities, delivered on the timeline of a fraction of a traditional three-month consulting engagement using AI-assisted research. *(2026-09)*
- **[Enterprise AI doesn't fail one review. It fails four.](./2026-09-ai-roi-that-survives.md)** — the multi-executive gate (CFO / CIO / CRM leader / CMO / HR) that quietly decides most enterprise AI investments, and the systematic methodology I've used across 10+ engagements to answer every seat at the table. *(2026-09)*
- **[ISMS from both sides of the table](./2026-09-isms-both-sides.md)** — Korea's government-mandated security certification quietly decides most enterprise AI adoption here. I've sat in both seats — as the business owner KISA questioned at adidas Korea, and as the Salesforce SE drafting responses for ~20 enterprise customers. *(2026-09)*
- **[I built a Super Mario–style game to teach enterprise customers how to use AI](./2026-08-customer-zero-award.md)** — how a gamified workshop tool won my region's Customer Zero Award, and why gamification is a serious answer to the AI-enablement problem, not a gimmick. *(2026-08)*
